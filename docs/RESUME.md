# Resume and interview wording

**CommerceOps AI — Human-supervised commerce automation**

- Built six commerce workflows with Python/FastAPI, n8n, structured Gemini outputs and SQL persistence, separating policy checks, human approvals and external action dispatch.
- Integrated Shopify Admin GraphQL and signed product webhooks, Slack review callbacks, HubSpot Tasks and Google Sheets; verified synthetic live flows with independent remote readbacks.
- Implemented duplicate suppression, bounded read/model retries, durable decision/outbox queues and ambiguous-write holds; validated behavior with 65 automated tests and a 161-event deterministic fixture evaluation.

Use publicly accessible repository and reviewer-page URLs only after publication. The local files and temporary backend tunnel are not suitable resume links. The reviewer page will host the recordings and evidence without needing the backend running.

In an interview, distinguish the two synthetic live samples from the six-workflow real-model smoke sample and the larger fixture evaluation. The Shopify store is not a Partner development store. Do not describe this as production Shopify employment, automated customer email delivery, measured revenue impact, time savings, model accuracy or a production reliability benchmark.
